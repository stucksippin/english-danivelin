Verbs · Past

Past Continuous

S + was/were + V-ing — процесс в конкретный момент прошлого

📐 Структура

+ Subject + was / were + V-ing
− Subject + wasn't / weren't + V-ing
? Was / Were + Subject + V-ing?

🔀 Утверждение · Отрицание · Вопрос

✚ Утверждение
S + was/were + V-ing
I was sleeping at 10 pm.
She was reading when he called.
They were playing outside.
We were watching TV.
− Отрицание
S + wasn't / weren't + V-ing
I wasn't sleeping.
She wasn't reading.
They weren't playing.
We weren't watching.
? Вопрос
Was / Were + S + V-ing?
Were you sleeping?
Was she reading?
Were they playing?
What were you doing?

💡 Когда использовать

Процесс в конкретный момент прошлого
At 8 pm, I was having dinner.
This time yesterday, she was flying to Paris.
What were you doing at noon?
Фон + событие (while / when)
While I was sleeping, the phone rang.
She was cooking when he arrived.
They were talking when the lights went out.
Два параллельных процесса
While she was cooking, he was reading.
I was working while they were playing.
Временный фон в нарративе
The sun was shining. Birds were singing. Then suddenly…
Everyone was waiting. Nobody was talking.

⚖️ Past Continuous vs Past Simple

Past Continuous
While she was cooking, he came in.
фоновый процесс, прерван событием
vs
Past Simple
She cooked dinner and he came in.
два последовательных события

🕐 Сигнальные слова

while when at that moment at 10 pm this time yesterday all day long as

Запомни

I/He/She/It → was + V-ing We/You/They → were + V-ing while — параллельный процесс когда = when (событие прерывает процесс)
She was reading when he called.  ·  Were you sleeping at midnight?
