Present Perfect Continuous

S + have/has + been + V-ing — процесс начат в прошлом, длится сейчас

📐 Структура

+ Subject + have / has + been + V-ing
− Subject + haven't / hasn't + been + V-ing
? Have / Has + Subject + been + V-ing?

🔀 Утверждение · Отрицание · Вопрос

✚ Утверждение
S + have/has + been + V-ing
I have been working all day.
She has been studying for 3 hours.
They have been waiting since noon.
He has been running.
− Отрицание
S + haven't/hasn't + been + V-ing
I haven't been sleeping well.
She hasn't been eating much.
They haven't been working hard.
? Вопрос
How long + have/has + S + been + V-ing?
Have you been waiting long?
How long has she been working here?
Has he been running?

💡 Когда использовать

Длительность от прошлого до сейчас
I have been learning English for 2 years.
She has been living here since 2021.
We have been waiting for an hour.
Видимый результат недавней активности
Your eyes are red — have you been crying?
He's out of breath. He has been running.
The floor is wet. Someone has been mopping.

⚖️ Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

Present Perfect
I have read the book.
результат — книга прочитана (завершено)
vs
PP Continuous
I have been reading the book.
процесс — читаю уже какое-то время
Present Perfect
She has written 3 emails.
количество / результат
vs
PP Continuous
She has been writing emails.
занята процессом написания

🕐 Сигнальные слова

for since all day all morning how long recently lately

Запомни

have/has + been + V-ing акцент на длительности процесса for + период · since + точка PP → результат · PPC → процесс
I have been studying for 3 hours.  ·  How long have you been waiting?
