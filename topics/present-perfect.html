Verbs · Present

Present Perfect

S + have/has + V3 — прошлое действие с результатом в настоящем

📐 Структура

+ Subject + have / has + V3
− Subject + haven't / hasn't + V3
? Have / Has + Subject + V3?

🔀 Утверждение · Отрицание · Вопрос

✚ Утверждение
S + have/has + V3
I have finished my work.
She has gone home.
We have met before.
He has broken his phone.
− Отрицание
S + haven't / hasn't + V3
I haven't finished yet.
She hasn't called.
We haven't eaten.
He hasn't replied.
? Вопрос
Have / Has + S + V3?
Have you finished?
Has she called?
Have you ever been to Japan?
Has he eaten?

💡 Когда использовать

Результат в настоящем
I have lost my keys. (= нет при мне)
She has broken her leg. (= в гипсе)
He has eaten all the pizza. (= нет)
Жизненный опыт (ever / never)
I have been to Paris.
Have you ever tried sushi?
She has never seen snow.
Только что / уже (just / already / yet)
She has just arrived.
I have already eaten.
Have you finished yet?
Незавершённый период (for / since)
I have lived here for 5 years.
She has worked here since 2019.
We have known each other for ages.

⚖️ Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Present Perfect
I have seen that film.
нет конкретного времени — связь с настоящим
vs
Past Simple
I saw that film yesterday.
конкретное прошлое время → Past Simple
Present Perfect
She has lost her keys.
результат важен сейчас
vs
Past Simple
She lost her keys last Monday.
акцент на факте в прошлом

🕐 Сигнальные слова

just already yet ever never recently for since so far up to now this week this year
💡 for = продолжительность (for 2 years, for a long time)  ·  since = начальная точка (since 2020, since Monday, since I was a child)

Запомни

have/has + V3 I/You/We/They → have He/She/It → has just · already · yet · ever · never for + период · since + точка нет конкретного времени → PP, не PS!
I have just finished.  ·  Have you ever been to Paris?  ·  She hasn't arrived yet.
