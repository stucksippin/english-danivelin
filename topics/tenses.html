🌅 Present — Настоящее

Present Simple
Факты, привычки, регулярные действия, расписания
+ S + V1  /  He/She/It + V1+s/es
− S + do/does not + V1
? Do/Does + S + V1?
She works every Monday. / He doesn't drink coffee.
always usually often sometimes never every day
Present Continuous
Действие прямо сейчас, временный процесс или ближайший план
+ S + am/is/are + V-ing
− S + am/is/are + not + V-ing
? Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing?
I am reading right now. / She is not working today.
now right now at the moment currently today
Present Perfect
Прошлое действие с результатом или связью с настоящим
+ S + have/has + V3
− S + have/has + not + V3
? Have/Has + S + V3?
I have finished my work. / She has never been to Paris.
just already yet ever never for since
Present Perfect Continuous
Процесс начат в прошлом и продолжается до сих пор
+ S + have/has + been + V-ing
− S + have/has + not + been + V-ing
? Have/Has + S + been + V-ing?
I have been studying for 3 hours.
for since all day how long recently

🌆 Past — Прошедшее

Past Simple
Завершённое действие в определённый момент прошлого
+ S + V2 (V+ed для правильных)
− S + did not + V1
? Did + S + V1?
She worked late. / I went home yesterday.
yesterday last week ago in 2020 when
Past Continuous
Длящееся действие в конкретный момент прошлого
+ S + was/were + V-ing
− S + was/were + not + V-ing
? Was/Were + S + V-ing?
I was sleeping at 10 pm. / They were talking when I came.
at 5 pm yesterday while when all night
Past Perfect
Действие завершилось до другого прошлого события
+ S + had + V3
− S + had not (hadn't) + V3
? Had + S + V3?
She had left before I arrived.
before after already by the time when
Past Perfect Continuous
Длительность процесса до определённого прошлого момента
+ S + had + been + V-ing
− S + had not + been + V-ing
? Had + S + been + V-ing?
He had been waiting for 2 hours when she arrived.
for since before all day

🌄 Future — Будущее

Future Simple
Спонтанное решение, предсказание, обещание или просьба
+ S + will + V1
− S + will not (won't) + V1
? Will + S + V1?
I will call you tomorrow. / She won't come.
tomorrow next week soon one day in the future
Future Continuous
Длящееся действие в конкретный момент будущего
+ S + will + be + V-ing
− S + will + not + be + V-ing
? Will + S + be + V-ing?
I will be sleeping at midnight. / She will be working then.
at this time tomorrow at noon this time next week
Future Perfect
Действие завершится к определённому моменту в будущем
+ S + will + have + V3
− S + will + not + have + V3
? Will + S + have + V3?
I will have finished by 5 pm.
by by the time before in X years
Future Perfect Continuous
Длительность процесса к определённому моменту будущего
+ S + will + have + been + V-ing
− S + will + not + have + been + V-ing
? Will + S + have + been + V-ing?
By June, I will have been learning English for 2 years.
by for by the time

Запомни структуру

Simple = V1 / V2 / will+V Continuous = be + V-ing Perfect = have/had + V3 Perfect Continuous = have/had + been + V-ing Present → am/is/are Past → was/were · had Future → will
Continuous = процесс  ·  Perfect = результат / до события  ·  Perfect Continuous = длительность процесса
