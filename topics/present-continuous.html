Verbs · Present

Present Continuous

S + am/is/are + V-ing — действие прямо сейчас или временный процесс

📐 Структура

+ Subject + am / is / are + V-ing
− Subject + am / is / are + not + V-ing
? Am / Is / Are + Subject + V-ing?

🔀 Утверждение · Отрицание · Вопрос

✚ Утверждение
S + am/is/are + V-ing
I am working now.
She is reading a book.
They are playing outside.
He is sleeping.
− Отрицание
S + 'm not / isn't / aren't + V-ing
I am not working.
She isn't reading.
They aren't playing.
He isn't sleeping.
? Вопрос
Am / Is / Are + S + V-ing?
Are you working?
Is she reading?
Are they playing?
Is he sleeping?

💡 Когда использовать

Действие прямо сейчас
Look! She is dancing.
I am writing an email right now.
Listen! He is singing.
Временный процесс (не прямо сейчас)
I am reading a great book this week.
She is staying with her sister this month.
They are building a new house.
Запланированное будущее
We are meeting them tomorrow.
She is flying to London next week.
I am having dinner with Tom tonight.
Раздражающая привычка (always)
He is always losing his keys!
She is always interrupting me!
They are always complaining!

⚠️ V-ing — правила написания

Большинство → просто +ing
work → working
play → playing
read → reading
Немая -e → убираем
come → coming
write → writing
make → making
CVC → удваиваем согл.
run → running
sit → sitting
swim → swimming
⚠️ Stative verbs — глаголы состояния, которые не используются в Continuous: know, believe, want, need, see, hear, love, hate, prefer, understand, seem, contain, belong.

🕐 Сигнальные слова

Запомни

am/is/are + V-ing I → am · He/She/It → is · We/You/They → are сейчас / временно / запланировано stative verbs → не используем!
