Verbs · Present

Present Simple

Факты, привычки, расписания — самое базовое время

📐 Структура

+ Subject + V1  /  He / She / It + V1 + s/es
− Subject + don't + V1  /  He / She / It + doesn't + V1
? Do / Does + Subject + V1?

🔀 Утверждение · Отрицание · Вопрос

✚ Утверждение
S + V1 / V1+s
I work every day.
She works in a bank.
They play football.
He goes to school.
− Отрицание
S + don't / doesn't + V1
I don't work on Sunday.
She doesn't drive.
They don't eat meat.
He doesn't like coffee.
? Вопрос
Do / Does + S + V1?
Do you work here?
Does she drive?
Do they like it?
Does he know?

💡 Когда использовать

Факты и общие истины
Water boils at 100°C.
The Earth orbits the Sun.
Cats eat meat.
Привычки и регулярные действия
She drinks coffee every morning.
I go to the gym on Mondays.
He reads before bed.
Расписание и программы
The train leaves at 9 pm.
The film starts at 8.
School begins in September.
Постоянные ситуации
I live in Moscow.
She works for Google.
He speaks three languages.

⚠️ He / She / It — окончания

Большинство → +s
work → works
play → plays
live → lives
-s/-sh/-ch/-x/-o → +es
go → goes
watch → watches
fix → fixes
Согл. + y → -ies
study → studies
fly → flies
carry → carries
⚠️ После doesn't глагол всегда в начальной форме: She doesn't work (не doesn't works).

🕐 Сигнальные слова

always usually often sometimes rarely never every day every week on Mondays in the morning at weekends twice a week

Запомни

I/You/We/They → V1 He/She/It → V1+s/es − → don't / doesn't + V1 ? → Do / Does + S + V1? doesn't → глагол БЕЗ -s
She works every day.  ·  Does she work on Sunday?  ·  She doesn't work on Sunday.
